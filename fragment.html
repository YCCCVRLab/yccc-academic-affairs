I Need Help

Email CTE Support

For CTE questions, the current site directs faculty to ycccCTESupport@mainecc.edu or Room 203. Academic Affairs can also be reached through Dr. Anne Crampton at acrampton@mainecc.edu.

Use the YCCC Syllabus Template V6 — ADA Compliant Version or visit Syllabus Creation.

Start with Brightspace Support, the Online Teaching page, or Brightspace.

Use the Early Alert Form. For broader barriers such as food insecurity, transportation, or mental health concerns, use the Care Team Link.

Contact the Center for Teaching Excellence at ycccCTESupport@mainecc.edu. The current CTE page also lists JJ Dufort, Chris Watkinson, M. Wendy West, and John Barr as team members.
